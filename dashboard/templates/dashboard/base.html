Frequently Asked Questions

The thresholds define the safe operating range for your agricultural environment. If a sensor reading falls outside of these boundaries (e.g., getting too hot, too dry, or detecting dangerous gases), the system will automatically trigger a warning or critical alert card, blinking red to notify you immediately.

You can dynamically adjust your thresholds by clicking the "Filter" button located just above the trend graphs on the dashboard. Update the minimum or maximum values as needed, and click save. The system will start evaluating the new rules immediately.

You can export your database records directly from the dashboard. Locate the "Export CSV" button above the graphs. Select the specific date you want to analyze using the date picker next to it, then click export. All readings recorded on that day will be downloaded securely as a spreadsheet.

If the system indicates an Offline status, it means the dashboard hasn't received data from the ESP32 microcontroller recently. Ensure the physical sensors and the ESP32 board are powered on, connected to the correct Wi-Fi network, and that no firewall is blocking port 8000.

The dot next to "System Status" provides a quick glance at your IoT device's connection. A green dot indicates the system is "Online" and actively receiving live sensor data. A gray dot indicates the system is "Offline" and data may be delayed.

Currently, alert emails are sent automatically when sensor readings hit critical thresholds. To stop receiving them entirely, you can either broaden your sensor thresholds using the Filter menu so they trigger less frequently, or contact an administrator to disable email notifications for your specific account.
